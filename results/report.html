Speed — how long does a real person wait?

Time from starting an action to seeing the result. Judged against the well-known Nielsen usability limits: under 1 s feels instant, under 10 s keeps the user's attention. “Typical” is the median; “worst case” is the 95th percentile (19 of 20 runs are faster).

User actionTypical waitWorst caseVerdict
Doctor: write & issue a prescription2.0 s2.3 sacceptable < 10 s (attention limit)

Of the doctor's wait, the actual cryptographic/blockchain work (on-chain proof verification) is only 343 ms (17.5%) — the rest is ordinary database/record-keeping. And it stays constant regardless of scale.
