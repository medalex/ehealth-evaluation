On-chain cost — how expensive is each blockchain operation?

“Gas” is the standard unit of on-chain computation — think of it as the price of a transaction. Lower is cheaper. For scale, one Ethereum block holds about 30,000,000 gas, so every operation below is a tiny fraction of a single block.
